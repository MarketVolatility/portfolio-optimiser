How each mandate calculates Optimized Weight

Every asset gets a raw score under whichever mandate is selected, then each asset's weight = its score ÷ the sum of all scores in the list, so weights always total 100%.

Mandate Score formula
Tactical Score = (Implied Upside × 100) + (Revenue Growth × 0.3) + a bonus of (Beta − 1) × 5 if Beta > 1. Ranked primarily by raw upside to target price, with modest credit for growth momentum and for carrying above-market volatility.
Conservative Score = stability bonus (+60 Ultra-high, +35 High) + 120 ÷ (P/E + 1) + (Net Margin × 0.5) − (D/E Ratio × 8) + beta adjustment (rewards Beta < 1, penalizes Beta > 1) + (Free Cash Flow positive: +15, negative: −15) + PEG bonus of (2 − PEG) × 5 when 0 < PEG < 2. Favors stable, profitable, low-leverage, low-volatility, cash-generative businesses.
Balanced Score = (ROA × 1.2) + (Implied Upside × 80) + (Revenue Growth × 0.4) + (Net Margin × 0.3) + PEG bonus of max(0, 3 − PEG) × 4 − (D/E Ratio × 3). Blends efficiency, growth, reasonably-priced valuation, and modest leverage discipline.
Aggressive Score = (Implied Upside × 180) + (ROA × 0.8) + (Revenue Growth × 0.6) + a bonus of min(Cash Runway, 3) × 6 specifically when Free Cash Flow is negative and Runway < 99999 years (rewards speculative, cash-burning names for having enough runway to survive) + a bonus of (Beta − 1.5) × 8 if Beta > 1.5. Leans hardest into growth, upside, and volatility.

Every score has a floor of 0.1 so no asset can reach a zero or negative weight. Custom parameters you've added yourself are display/editable only — they aren't included in any of the formulas above.

Parameter glossary (A–Z)

Definitions for every option in the "+/- Parameter" dropdown on both the Portfolio Lists table and the Past Purchases table, in alphabetical order — plus the fixed Portfolio Lists columns (like Current Price, ROA, Beta, Stability) that come from the Sample List and aren't re-offered as dropdown options there since they already exist as columns. Most of those fixed columns ARE offered as manual-entry presets in the Past Purchases dropdown, since that table has no fixed columns of its own; entries below say which dropdown(s) each parameter appears in.

Parameter Definition
30-Day Volatility (%)The standard deviation of daily price returns over the trailing 30 days; higher values mean more erratic price swings.
52-Week High ($)The highest trading price of the stock over the past 52 weeks.
52-Week Low ($)The lowest trading price of the stock over the past 52 weeks.
Actual Upside (%)Computed automatically (read-only): (Current Price − your Average Purchase Price) ÷ Average Purchase Price × 100 — your real gain/loss on the position. Requires the "Average Purchase Price ($)" column to be added and filled in; shows 0% until then.
Analyst Rating (Consensus)The average or most common recommendation (e.g. Buy, Hold, Sell) among equity analysts covering the stock.
Asset TurnoverTotal revenue divided by total assets; measures how efficiently a company uses its assets to generate sales.
Average Purchase Price ($)Manual entry: the average price per share you actually paid, for your own cost-basis tracking. Not fetched from any source.
Average Volume (M)The average number of shares (in millions) traded per day over a recent period.
BetaFixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table; also offered as a manual-entry preset in the Past Purchases dropdown (which has no fixed columns of its own). A measure of the stock’s price volatility relative to the overall market — 1.0 moves with the market, above 1.0 is more volatile, below 1.0 is less.
Cash Runway (yr)Fixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table; also offered as a manual-entry preset in the Past Purchases dropdown (which has no fixed columns of its own). The number of years a company could keep operating at its current cash-burn rate before running out of cash.
Company NameFixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table (always present, not added via the dropdown). Not offered as a Past Purchases preset since the Asset column there already serves as the row’s identity.
Country / RegionThe primary country or geographic region where the company is headquartered or listed.
Current PriceFixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table; also offered as a manual-entry preset in the Past Purchases dropdown (which has no fixed columns of its own). The asset’s most recent trading price. On the main table this is live-fetchable via Finnhub; in Past Purchases it’s a manual entry, useful for recording the price at the time you’re logging the row.
Current RatioCurrent assets divided by current liabilities; measures ability to cover short-term obligations.
Current/Target Price (%)Computed automatically (read-only): Current Price ÷ Target Price × 100. Below 100% implies upside remaining; above 100% means the stock has passed its target.
D/E RatioFixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table; also offered as a manual-entry preset in the Past Purchases dropdown (which has no fixed columns of its own). Debt-to-equity: total debt divided by shareholder equity; higher values indicate more leverage.
Date PurchasedManual entry: the date you bought the position, via a calendar picker (defaults to today). Not fetched from any source.
Date SalePast Purchases only. The date you sold the position, via a calendar picker. Groups rows into the “Sales for month of X of Y” subtotals in the table’s footer, sorted most-recent month first.
Days Sales OutstandingThe average number of days a company takes to collect payment after a sale; lower is generally better.
Dividend Payout Ratio (%)The percentage of net income paid out to shareholders as dividends.
Dividend Yield (%)Annual dividend per share divided by current share price. Live-fetchable via Finnhub where available.
EmployeesThe approximate total number of full-time employees at the company.
Enterprise Value ($B)Market capitalization plus debt, minus cash; the theoretical cost to acquire the whole company.
EPS Diluted ($)Earnings per share assuming all convertible securities (options, warrants) are exercised.
EPS Growth (YoY%)Year-over-year percentage change in diluted earnings per share.
EV/EBITDAEnterprise Value ÷ EBITDA; a valuation multiple that adjusts for capital structure and non-cash expenses.
EV/RevenueEnterprise Value ÷ annual revenue; useful for comparing companies with different profitability levels.
FCF ($M)Fixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table; also offered as a manual-entry preset in the Past Purchases dropdown (which has no fixed columns of its own). Free cash flow in millions of dollars — operating cash flow minus capital expenditures.
Float (%)The percentage of total shares outstanding that are freely tradable by the public.
Forward P/EPrice ÷ projected (estimated) earnings per share for the next fiscal year, rather than trailing actual earnings.
Gross Margin (%)Gross profit (revenue minus cost of goods sold) divided by revenue.
Implied UpsideFixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table (always present, not added via the dropdown). Computed automatically on the main table from Current Price vs. Target Price; feeds the Balanced and Aggressive scoring formulas above. Not offered in Past Purchases — it depends on the optimizer’s scoring engine rather than standing alone per row.
IndustryThe specific industry classification within the company's broader sector.
Insider Ownership (%)The percentage of shares held by company executives, directors, and other insiders.
Institutional Ownership (%)The percentage of shares held by mutual funds, pension funds, and other institutional investors.
Interest Coverage RatioOperating income (EBIT) divided by interest expense; measures ability to service debt.
Inventory TurnoverCost of goods sold divided by average inventory; how many times inventory is sold and replaced per period.
IPO DateThe date the company's shares first began trading on a public exchange.
MACD SignalA qualitative read (e.g. Bullish, Bearish, Neutral) from the Moving Average Convergence Divergence technical indicator.
Market Cap ($B)Current share price × total shares outstanding, in billions of dollars. Live-fetchable via Finnhub.
Net Margin (%)Fixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table; also offered as a manual-entry preset in the Past Purchases dropdown (which has no fixed columns of its own). Net income divided by revenue.
Number of Analysts CoveringThe count of equity research analysts who publish estimates or ratings on the stock.
Operating Margin (%)Operating income divided by revenue.
Optimized Weight AllocationFixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table (always present, not added via the dropdown). Computed automatically for the active Portfolio List as a whole (not per-row), based on the selected scoring strategy. Not applicable to Past Purchases.
P/E MultipleFixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table; also offered as a manual-entry preset in the Past Purchases dropdown (which has no fixed columns of its own). Share price divided by trailing earnings per share.
PEG RatioFixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table; also offered as a manual-entry preset in the Past Purchases dropdown (which has no fixed columns of its own). P/E ratio divided by earnings growth rate; below 1 is often considered undervalued relative to growth.
Price to Book (P/B)Share price divided by book value per share; compares market value to accounting net asset value.
Price to Sales (P/S)Market cap divided by revenue; a valuation multiple often used for early-stage or low-margin companies.
Quick Ratio(Current assets minus inventory) ÷ current liabilities; a stricter liquidity measure than the Current Ratio.
R&D Spend (% of Revenue)Research and development expenditure as a percentage of total revenue.
Return on Equity (ROE %)Net income divided by shareholder equity; profitability relative to shareholders' investment.
Return on Invested Capital (ROIC %)Net operating profit after tax divided by invested capital; efficiency of returns on deployed capital.
Rev Growth (YoY%)Fixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table; also offered as a manual-entry preset in the Past Purchases dropdown (which has no fixed columns of its own). Year-over-year percentage change in revenue.
ROA (%)Fixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table; also offered as a manual-entry preset in the Past Purchases dropdown (which has no fixed columns of its own). Return on assets: net income divided by total assets.
RSI (14-day)Relative Strength Index over 14 days (0–100); above 70 suggests overbought, below 30 suggests oversold.
Sale ProfitPast Purchases only. Computed automatically (read-only): Units Purchased × (Selling Price − Average Purchase Price). Requires “Units Purchased,” “Average Purchase Price ($),” and “Selling Price” columns all present and a non-zero Selling Price entered; shows 0 until then. Feeds the monthly “Sales for month of X of Y” subtotals and the “Total Sale Profit to date” footer row.
SectorThe broad economic sector the company operates in (e.g. Technology, Healthcare, Financials).
Selling PricePast Purchases only. Manual entry: the price per share/unit you sold at. Not fetched from any source.
Shares Outstanding (M)The total number of shares (in millions) currently issued and held by all shareholders.
Short Interest (%)The percentage of outstanding shares that have been sold short by investors betting on a price decline.
StabilityFixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table; also offered as a manual-entry preset in the Past Purchases dropdown (which has no fixed columns of its own). A qualitative rating of business durability (Ultra-high, High, Med, or Low) used in the scoring formulas above. On the main table it’s a dropdown; in Past Purchases it’s free text, since custom parameters there don’t support the dropdown-style rating.
Target PriceFixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table; also offered as a manual-entry preset in the Past Purchases dropdown (which has no fixed columns of its own). The analyst or your own price target for the asset, used to compute Current/Target Price (%) and Implied Upside.
Units PurchasedManual entry: the number of shares/units you actually hold, for your own position tracking. Not fetched from any source.

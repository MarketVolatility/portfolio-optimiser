How each mandate calculates Optimized Weight

Every asset gets a raw score under whichever mandate is selected, then each asset's weight = its score ÷ the sum of all scores in the list, so weights always total 100%.

Mandate Score formula
Tactical Score = (Implied Upside × 100) + (Revenue Growth × 0.3) + a bonus of (Beta − 1) × 5 if Beta > 1. Ranked primarily by raw upside to target price, with modest credit for growth momentum and for carrying above-market volatility.
Conservative Score = stability bonus (+60 Ultra-high, +35 High) + 120 ÷ (P/E + 1) + (Net Margin × 0.5) − (D/E Ratio × 8) + beta adjustment (rewards Beta < 1, penalizes Beta > 1) + (Free Cash Flow positive: +15, negative: −15) + PEG bonus of (2 − PEG) × 5 when 0 < PEG < 2. Favors stable, profitable, low-leverage, low-volatility, cash-generative businesses.
Balanced Score = (ROA × 1.2) + (Implied Upside × 80) + (Revenue Growth × 0.4) + (Net Margin × 0.3) + PEG bonus of max(0, 3 − PEG) × 4 − (D/E Ratio × 3). Blends efficiency, growth, reasonably-priced valuation, and modest leverage discipline.
Aggressive Score = (Implied Upside × 180) + (ROA × 0.8) + (Revenue Growth × 0.6) + a bonus of min(Cash Runway, 3) × 6 specifically when Free Cash Flow is negative and Runway < 99999 years (rewards speculative, cash-burning names for having enough runway to survive) + a bonus of (Beta − 1.5) × 8 if Beta > 1.5. Leans hardest into growth, upside, and volatility.

Every score has a floor of 0.1 so no asset can reach a zero or negative weight. Custom parameters you've added yourself are display/editable only — they aren't included in any of the formulas above.

Parameter glossary (A–Z)

Definitions for every option in the "+/- Parameter" dropdown — the same A–Z list on both the Portfolio Lists table and the Past Purchases table — plus the fixed Portfolio Lists columns (like Current Price, ROA, Beta, Stability) that come from the Sample List. Those fixed columns also appear as dropdown options (so they're documented and, on Past Purchases, addable as manual-entry fields), but adding one on the Portfolio table itself is blocked since the fixed column already exists there; entries below say when that applies.

Parameter Definition
30-Day Volatility (%)The standard deviation of daily price returns over the trailing 30 days; higher values mean more erratic price swings.
52-Week High ($)The highest trading price of the stock over the past 52 weeks.
52-Week Low ($)The lowest trading price of the stock over the past 52 weeks.
Actual Upside (%)Computed automatically (read-only): (Current Price − your Average Purchase Price) ÷ Average Purchase Price × 100 — your real gain/loss on the position. Requires the "Average Purchase Price ($)" column to be added and filled in; shows 0% until then.
Analyst Rating (Consensus)The average or most common recommendation (e.g. Buy, Hold, Sell) among equity analysts covering the stock.
Asset TurnoverTotal revenue divided by total assets; measures how efficiently a company uses its assets to generate sales.
Average Purchase Price ($)Manual entry: the average price per share you actually paid, for your own cost-basis tracking. Not fetched from any source.
Average Volume (M)The average number of shares (in millions) traded per day over a recent period.
BetaFixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table. A measure of the stock’s price volatility relative to the overall market — 1.0 moves with the market, above 1.0 is more volatile, below 1.0 is less.
Book ValueComputed automatically (read-only): Units Purchased × Average Purchase Price — what the position cost, regardless of whether it's been sold yet. Requires "Units Purchased" and "Average Purchase Price ($)" columns both present and non-zero; shows $0.00 until then. Feeds the "Total Current Book Value" footer row, which sums this across all rows with no Selling Price entered (i.e. not yet sold).

The number of years a company could keep operating at its current cash-burn rate before running out of cash.
Company NameFixed column on the main Portfolio Lists table (always present, not added via the dropdown). Not offered as a Past Purchases preset since the Asset column there already serves as the row’s identity.

The analyst or your own price target for the asset, used to compute Current/Consensus Target Price (%) and Implied Upside.
Country / RegionThe primary country or geographic region where the company is headquartered or listed.

Past Purchases viewing: —

Freeform tracking table.
  • Starts with a common set of columns (Realized Gain, Unrealized Gain, Book Value, Date Purchased, Units Purchased, and more) — add, remove, or rename them anytime via "+/- Parameter" to fit what you track.
  • Recording sales: keep each purchase on its own row (Units Purchased, Average Purchase Price, Date Purchased), then add each sale as a separate row with the "Sell" button under a ticker (Units Sold, Selling Price, Date Sale). Each sale's Realized Gain is matched against that ticker's purchases on this list, using the "Cost basis" setting (Average cost or FIFO). A row with both purchase and sale on one line still works too.
  • Import the full asset list from any Portfolio List, or copy every row from another Past Purchases list, using "Import list" below.
  • Data flows one way, from Portfolio Lists into Past Purchases: adding/importing a ticker carries over Units Purchased, Average Purchase Price, and Date Purchased, plus fills any other column here that matches a Portfolio Lists column by name.
  • That pull only happens once, when a row is added — use "↻ Refresh from Portfolio Lists" below anytime, including after "Fetch live data" up on the Portfolio Lists table, to re-pull the latest values into rows already here.
This table lays purchases and sales out as FIFO-matched lots: a purchase splits across several rows here if it fed more than one sale, and a sale splits across several rows if it drew from more than one purchase — always oldest-purchase-first (FIFO), even when "Cost basis" above is set to Average cost, since Average cost blends prices across lots and has nothing clean to split into rows like this. Every cell here is editable and writes back to the real purchase or sale it belongs to. In the ticker cell: ↑ ↓ reorders that ticker's whole block of rows, Sell records a new sale, and × deletes; "Held: x/y" shows how many units of that purchase are still unsold. The "Total current market and book value" (shown as two totals — Book Value and Current Market Value, each in its own column), "Total Realized Gain to date", and monthly Realized Gain totals below are computed from these FIFO-matched rows.

    Methodology and Glossary

    How the Portfolio scores each asset, and what every column means. Search, or jump to a part.

    How each mandate calculates Optimized Weight

    Every asset gets a raw score under whichever mandate is selected, then each asset's weight = its score ÷ the sum of all scores in the list, so weights always total 100%.

    Mandate Score formula
    Tactical Score = (Implied Upside × 100) + (Revenue Growth × 0.3) + a bonus of (Beta − 1) × 5 if Beta > 1. Ranked primarily by raw upside to target price, with modest credit for growth momentum and for carrying above-market volatility.
    Conservative Score = stability bonus (+60 Ultra-high, +35 High) + 120 ÷ (P/E + 1) + (Net Margin × 0.5) − (D/E Ratio × 8) + beta adjustment (rewards Beta < 1, penalizes Beta > 1) + (Free Cash Flow positive: +15, negative: −15) + PEG bonus of (2 − PEG) × 5 when 0 < PEG < 2. Favors stable, profitable, low-leverage, low-volatility, cash-generative businesses.
    Balanced Score = (ROA × 1.2) + (Implied Upside × 80) + (Revenue Growth × 0.4) + (Net Margin × 0.3) + PEG bonus of max(0, 3 − PEG) × 4 − (D/E Ratio × 3). Blends efficiency, growth, reasonably-priced valuation, and modest leverage discipline.
    Aggressive Score = (Implied Upside × 180) + (ROA × 0.8) + (Revenue Growth × 0.6) + a bonus of min(Cash Runway, 3) × 6 specifically when Free Cash Flow is negative and Runway < 99999 years (rewards speculative, cash-burning names for having enough runway to survive) + a bonus of (Beta − 1.5) × 8 if Beta > 1.5. Leans hardest into growth, upside, and volatility.

    Every score has a floor of 0.1 so no asset can reach a zero or negative weight. Custom parameters you've added yourself are display/editable only — they aren't included in any of the formulas above.

    Parameter glossary (A–Z) — shared definitions

    Definitions for every parameter whose meaning is the same wherever it appears, A–Z. A parameter whose meaning differs between tables is NOT listed here — look it up in that table's own glossary further down (Portfolio, Past Purchases, Sales Strategy, Buy Strategy).

    Parameter Definition
    30-Day Volatility (%)The standard deviation of daily price returns over the trailing 30 days; higher values mean more erratic price swings.
    52-Week High ($)The highest trading price of the stock over the past 52 weeks.
    52-Week Low ($)The lowest trading price of the stock over the past 52 weeks.
    Actual Upside (%)Computed automatically (read-only): (Current Price − your Average Purchase Price) ÷ Average Purchase Price × 100 — your real gain/loss on the position. Requires the "Average Purchase Price ($)" column to be added and filled in; shows 0% until then.
    Analyst Rating (Consensus)The average or most common recommendation (e.g. Buy, Hold, Sell) among equity analysts covering the stock.
    Asset TurnoverTotal revenue divided by total assets; measures how efficiently a company uses its assets to generate sales.
    Average Volume (M)The average number of shares (in millions) traded per day over a recent period.
    BetaA measure of the stock’s price volatility relative to the overall market — 1.0 moves with the market, above 1.0 is more volatile, below 1.0 is less.
    Cash & Equivalents ($M)Cash and cash-equivalent balance-sheet holdings, in millions of dollars — one of Cash Runway (yr)'s two inputs. Live-fetchable via "Fetch live data" (SEC EDGAR first, Alpha Vantage as a fallback).
    Cash Runway (yr)Computed automatically (read-only): Cash & Equivalents ($M) ÷ Operating Expenses ($M) — a "zero revenue" runway, i.e. how many years current cash would cover total annual operating expenses alone, regardless of revenue. Shows "N/A" until both inputs are on file.
    Company NameThe company's full name.
    Consensus Target PriceThe analyst or your own price target for the asset, used to compute Current/Consensus Target Price (%) and Implied Upside.
    Country / RegionThe primary country or geographic region where the company is headquartered or listed.

    Current/Consensus Target Price (%)Computed automatically (read-only): Current Price ÷ Consensus Target Price × 100. Below 100% implies upside remaining; above 100% means the stock has passed its target.

    Days Sales OutstandingThe average number of days a company takes to collect payment after a sale; lower is generally better.
    Dividend Payout Ratio (%)The percentage of net income paid out to shareholders as dividends.
    Dividend Yield (%)Annual dividend per share divided by current share price. Live-fetchable via Finnhub where available.
    EmployeesThe approximate total number of full-time employees at the company.
    Enterprise Value ($B)Market capitalization plus debt, minus cash; the theoretical cost to acquire the whole company.
    EPS Diluted ($)Earnings per share assuming all convertible securities (options, warrants) are exercised.
    EPS Growth (YoY%)Year-over-year percentage change in diluted earnings per share.
    EV/EBITDAEnterprise Value ÷ EBITDA; a valuation multiple that adjusts for capital structure and non-cash expenses.
    EV/RevenueEnterprise Value ÷ annual revenue; useful for comparing companies with different profitability levels.

    Float (%)The percentage of total shares outstanding that are freely tradable by the public.
    Forward P/EPrice ÷ projected (estimated) earnings per share for the next fiscal year, rather than trailing actual earnings.
    Gross Margin (%)Gross profit (revenue minus cost of goods sold) divided by revenue.
    Implied UpsideComputed automatically from Current Price vs. Consensus Target Price; feeds the Balanced and Aggressive scoring formulas above.
    IndustryThe specific industry classification within the company's broader sector.
    Insider Ownership (%)The percentage of shares held by company executives, directors, and other insiders.
    Institutional Ownership (%)The percentage of shares held by mutual funds, pension funds, and other institutional investors.
    Interest Coverage RatioOperating income (EBIT) divided by interest expense; measures ability to service debt.
    Inventory TurnoverCost of goods sold divided by average inventory; how many times inventory is sold and replaced per period.

    MACD SignalA qualitative read (e.g. Bullish, Bearish, Neutral) from the Moving Average Convergence Divergence technical indicator.

    Number of Analysts CoveringThe count of equity research analysts who publish estimates or ratings on the stock.
    Operating Expenses ($M)Total annual operating expenses, in millions of dollars — the other input to Cash Runway (yr). Live-fetchable via "Fetch live data" (SEC EDGAR first, Alpha Vantage as a fallback).

    Price to Sales (P/S)Market cap divided by revenue; a valuation multiple often used for early-stage or low-margin companies.

    R&D Spend (% of Revenue)Research and development expenditure as a percentage of total revenue.

    Return on Invested Capital (ROIC %)Net operating profit after tax divided by invested capital; efficiency of returns on deployed capital.
    Rev Growth (YoY%)Year-over-year percentage change in revenue.
    ROA (%)Return on assets: net income divided by total assets.
    RSI (14-day)Relative Strength Index over 14 days (0–100); above 70 suggests overbought, below 30 suggests oversold.
    SectorThe broad economic sector the company operates in (e.g. Technology, Healthcare, Financials).
    Shares Outstanding (M)The total number of shares (in millions) currently issued and held by all shareholders.

    Portfolio table — definitions

    Where a parameter means something different here than in Past Purchases, Sales Strategy or Buy Strategy, this table gives the Portfolio meaning.

    Parameter Definition in this table

    Current PriceThe asset's most recent trading price. Live-fetchable via Finnhub or Alpha Vantage (two independent sources — see Fetch Live Data), or typed as a manual override. Drives Implied Upside, Actual Upside (%) and the scoring formulas.
    Date PurchasedManual entry: the date you bought the position, as DD/MM/YYYY. A plain note on this table — it does not drive any calculation here.
    Date SaleManual entry: the date you sold, as DD/MM/YYYY. A plain field on this table — no monthly grouping happens here (that is Past Purchases only).
    Selling PriceManual entry: the price per share you sold at. Used only for Missed Gain %. Not fetched from any source.

    Past Purchases (Lot Matching) table — definitions

    The Past Purchases and Planning section's Lot Matching table. Purchases and sales are matched oldest-first (FIFO), separately for each ticker or option contract.

    Parameter Definition in this table
    Average Purchase Price ($)Manual entry on a purchase row: the price per unit you paid for THAT lot (per contract for an option). Each lot keeps its own price — the FIFO engine matches sales against lots oldest-first, and Book Value, Realized Gain and Unrealized Gain all use it. Not fetched from any source.
    Book ValueComputed automatically (read-only): units still held × that lot's Average Purchase Price — the cost of what you still own from that FIFO-matched lot (fully sold = no Book Value; a sold lot's value is tracked as Realized Gain instead). Options are × the contract multiplier. Shown in light blue while units are still held. The "Total current market and book value" row below the table sums it across every held lot.
    Current Market ValueComputed automatically (read-only): units still held × Current Price — what that FIFO-matched lot is worth right now (fully sold = none; its value becomes Realized Gain). Options are × the contract multiplier. Book Value (what it cost) + Unrealized Gain (paper gain/loss) = Current Market Value.
    Current PriceStocks: the live or overridden price for the ticker (same source as the Portfolio table). Call options: one price per contract, typed in or fetched from Marketdata.app, and stored for that contract. Every row of the same ticker/contract shows the same price, and Sales Strategy and Buy Strategy read it from here.
    Date PurchasedManual entry: the date of that purchase, typed as DD/MM/YYYY. Starts blank (never assumed to be today). It sets the oldest-first order in which FIFO matches sales, and the default newest-first sort of the table.
    Date SaleThe date of that sale (DD/MM/YYYY; 00/00/0000 = no sale date). It groups rows into the "Realized Gain for month of X of Y" subtotals in the table footer, most recent month first. An unsold call option past its Expiration Date is recorded automatically as a $0 sale on that date.
    Realized GainComputed automatically (read-only) per FIFO-matched lot: units sold × (Selling Price − that lot's Average Purchase Price), always oldest-purchase-first. Options are × the contract multiplier. Example: buy 10 @ $2, sell 5 @ $3 (+$5), sell 5 @ $5 (+$15) = $20. The monthly subtotals and the "Total Realized Gain to date" row total it.
    Selling PriceManual entry on a sale row: the price per unit you actually sold at (per contract for an option). Feeds Realized Gain and Missed Gain %. A confirmed Sales Strategy row writes its planned Selling Price here.
    StabilityFree text on this table (custom columns here have no dropdown). It is a plain note and does not feed any scoring.
    To Buy PriceManual entry: a price threshold you set for the asset. When present beside Current Price, Current Price turns emerald on that row once it falls to or below it. Not used by Buy Strategy, which has its own Buying Price.
    Units PurchasedManual entry on a purchase row: how many units that purchase added (contracts for an option). Total Units Holding in Sales Strategy and Buy Strategy is built from these minus what FIFO has matched to sales. A confirmed Buy Strategy row writes its Units to Buy here.
    Units SoldManual entry on a sale row: how many units that sale takes from this ticker's purchases, matched oldest-first. On a row that also has Units Purchased it records a partial sale of that row (blank = the whole row was sold). A confirmed Sales Strategy row writes its Units to Sell here.
    Unrealized GainComputed automatically (read-only): the row's full Units Purchased × (Current Price − Average Purchase Price); options are × the contract multiplier. For units still held it is the real paper gain/loss. For units already sold it is hypothetical — what they would be worth today at the original buy price had you kept them — shown alongside Realized Gain for comparison. Not the same as Sales Strategy's Unrealised Capital Gain.

    Sales Strategy table — definitions

    Planned (draft) sales. Nothing here is real until you click Confirm Sale.

    Parameter Definition in this table
    Average Purchase Price of Units LeftComputed (read-only): the FIFO blended cost per unit of the lots that would still be held after this row AND every row above it for the same ticker/contract take their Units to Sell (oldest lots go first). "—" when nothing would be left.
    Current PriceRead-only: the ticker's (or option contract's) current price as held on Past Purchases/Portfolio — it cannot be edited here. "—" if no price is known yet.
    Sale Realized DateHolds the "Confirm Sale" button. Confirming asks for the sale date, adds a real sale row (Units Sold, Selling Price, Date Sale) to Past Purchases, and locks the row green with that date. Confirmed rows drop off after the Display window (default 28 days); the real sale stays on Past Purchases.
    Selling PriceTyped: the price per unit you plan to sell THIS batch at. Nothing is real until Confirm Sale, which writes it to Past Purchases as the sale's Selling Price. Feeds Unrealised Capital Gain.
    Total Units HoldingComputed from Past Purchases: the units of this ticker/contract you really hold right now. It falls when a sale is confirmed (here or on Past Purchases) and rises when more is bought, including through Buy Strategy's Confirm Buy.
    Units Pending PlanComputed: Total Units Holding − the Units to Sell in this row and every row above it for the same ticker/contract (a running total down the table, floored at 0, in the table's own order). Red = over-committed (you plan to sell more than you hold).
    Units to SellTyped: how many units you plan to sell in this batch (contracts for an option). Becomes the sale's Units Sold on Confirm Sale.
    Unrealised Capital GainComputed per row: (Selling Price − Average Purchase Price of Units Left) × Units to Sell, × the contract multiplier for an option. On the row that sells the LAST units (Units Pending Plan = 0) it uses the average purchase price of the units pending just before that row — the units it sells. "—" when the row has no Units to Sell or Selling Price, or is already confirmed (then the gain is realized). The first row of the table totals it for this table only.

    Buy Strategy table — definitions

    Planned (draft) buys. Nothing here is real until you click Confirm Buy.

    Parameter Definition in this table
    Buy Realized DateHolds the "Confirm Buy" button. Confirming asks for the purchase date and adds a real purchase row (Units Purchased, Average Purchase Price, Date Purchased — plus strike/expiration for an option) to Past Purchases, then locks the row green with that date. Confirmed rows drop off after the Display window (default 28 days); the real purchase stays on Past Purchases.
    Buying PriceTyped: the price per unit you plan to pay in this batch (per contract for an option). Becomes the purchase's Average Purchase Price on Confirm Buy.
    Buying Price vs Current (%)Computed: (Buying Price ÷ Current Price − 1) × 100. Negative (emerald) = you plan to buy below the current price; positive = above it. "—" without both prices.
    Current Average Purchase PriceComputed from Past Purchases: the FIFO blended cost per unit of the units you hold right now, before any plan. "—" if you hold none.
    Current PriceRead-only: the ticker's (or option contract's) current price as held on Past Purchases/Portfolio. "—" if none is known yet (common for a ticker you don't hold).
    New Average Purchase PriceComputed: the blended cost per unit after the plan — the lots left once Sales Strategy's planned sells are taken (oldest first), plus this row's buys and every buy row above it for the same ticker/contract. "—" until Units to Buy is entered.
    Planned CostComputed: Units to Buy × Buying Price, × the contract multiplier for an option. The first row of the table totals every still-draft row.
    Total Units HoldingComputed from Past Purchases: the units of this ticker/contract you really hold right now (0 for a ticker you don't hold yet).
    Units Holding After PlanComputed: Total Units Holding − Units Planned to Sell + the Units to Buy in this row and every row above it for the same ticker/contract (a running total down the table). "—" once the row is confirmed, because it is then part of Total Units Holding.
    Units Planned to SellComputed from Sales Strategy: the total Units to Sell across that ticker/contract's still-draft (unconfirmed) Sales Strategy rows.
    Units to BuyTyped: how many units you plan to buy in this batch (contracts for an option). Becomes the purchase's Units Purchased on Confirm Buy.
